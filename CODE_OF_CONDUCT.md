# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, give and accept constructive feedback, and keep discussion focused on the work. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not tolerated.

Report violations privately through GitHub's private reporting on the Security tab. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and ban contributors who break these rules.
